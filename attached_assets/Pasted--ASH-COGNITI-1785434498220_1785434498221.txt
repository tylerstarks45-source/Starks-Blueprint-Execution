2. Opportunity Classification Engine

Confidence

4. Opportunity Ranking Engine

Ranks every opportunity.

CHIEF ARCHITECT NOTE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Business Analysis explains reality.

Opportunity Discovery identifies value.

These responsibilities must never merge.

Opportunity Discovery is the executive lens that highlights where
attention should be focused. It does not decide what to do about
those opportunities—that responsibility belongs to Strategic
Simulation and Decision Intelligence in subsequent builds.

When implementation is complete:

STOP.

Wait for Build 018.
