PURPOSE

The Unified Business Process Engine establishes the canonical
representation of every repeatable business process executed inside
the platform.

Processes become first-class business assets.

Every future application executes standardized processes rather
than implementing independent workflow logic.

ASH reasons about outcomes, bottlenecks, dependencies,
handoffs, approvals, SLAs, and optimization opportunities.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

MISSION

Create one deterministic business process platform that supports
every operational function of a business.

CHIEF ARCHITECT NOTE

Business processes are one of the foundational domains of every
organization.

By establishing a single canonical process engine, future modules
such as Commerce, CRM, Marketing, HR, Finance, Support, AI
Workforce, and Operations will all execute against the same
business process architecture rather than implementing separate
workflow models.

This gives ASH a complete understanding of how work moves through
a business, allowing continuous optimization, bottleneck analysis,
and cross-domain orchestration.
