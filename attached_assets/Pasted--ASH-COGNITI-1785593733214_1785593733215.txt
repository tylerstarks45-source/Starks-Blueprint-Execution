PURPOSE

The Systems Dynamics Intelligence Engine enables ASH to reason
about entire interconnected systems rather than isolated
components.

Business Analysis evaluates businesses.

Technology Relationship Intelligence evaluates technologies.

Systems Dynamics evaluates how changes propagate through complete
systems.

This engine never executes work.

It models cause, effect, propagation, stability, leverage, and
system behavior.
