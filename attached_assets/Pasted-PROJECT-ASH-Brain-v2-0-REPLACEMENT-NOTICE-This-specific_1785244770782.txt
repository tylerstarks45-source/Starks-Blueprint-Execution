========================================================

PHASE 1.5A

BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE ARCHITECTURE

========================================================

MISSION

The Business Intelligence Engine is no longer simply a collection of business information.

It becomes the central intelligence layer responsible for understanding every business ASH manages.

It should know:

• what the business is
• why it exists
• how it makes money
• what stage it is in
• where it is going
• what problems exist
• what opportunities exist
• what should happen next

No other subsystem should need to reconstruct business context.

This engine owns that responsibility.

========================================================

PRIMARY RESPONSIBILITIES

The Business Intelligence Engine shall become responsible for:

• Business Identity
• Business State
• Business Context
• Business Lifecycle
• Business Health
• Business Objectives
• Business Intelligence
• Business Recommendations
• Business Relationships
• Business Analytics Context
• Business Strategy Context

========================================================
