PURPOSE

The Universal Canvas Engine establishes the shared visual editing
surface used by every builder inside the platform.

The canvas is not specific to websites.

The canvas is not specific to workflows.

The canvas represents a universal editing environment capable of
hosting any visual business asset.

Builders provide asset-specific behavior.

The canvas provides interaction.

MISSION
