CHIEF ARCHITECT NOTE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The Operational Planning Engine marks the transition from executive
reasoning into operational reasoning.

It converts approved capabilities into organized operational work,
while deliberately remaining separate from execution.

It never schedules work.

It never assigns personnel.

It never performs actions.

Its sole responsibility is to answer:

"How should the work itself be organized?"

Execution begins only after this layer.

This separation between Operational Planning and Execution is a
permanent architectural principle of the ASH Cognitive Operating
System.

When implementation is complete:

STOP.

Wait for Build 024.
