CHIEF ARCHITECT NOTE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The Executive Principle Learning Engine is the system that transforms
experience into reusable executive wisdom.

It does not optimize behavior.

It does not rewrite cognition.

Instead, it discovers durable principles supported by repeated
evidence across many Executive Cases.

By introducing the Executive Principle Graph, ASH begins to organize
wisdom as a connected body of knowledge rather than a collection of
isolated lessons.

This allows future reasoning to navigate relationships between
principles, recognize contradictions, specialize advice by business
domain, and evolve a coherent executive worldview over time.

Learning is the first stage where ASH becomes more capable because of
its own accumulated experience.

This architectural boundary is permanent.

When implementation is complete:

STOP.

Wait for Build 014.
