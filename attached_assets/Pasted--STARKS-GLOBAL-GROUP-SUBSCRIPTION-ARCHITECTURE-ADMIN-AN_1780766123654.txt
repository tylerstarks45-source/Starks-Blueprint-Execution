# STARKS GLOBAL GROUP — SUBSCRIPTION ARCHITECTURE & ADMIN ANALYTICS SYSTEM

## OBJECTIVE

Redesign the subscription system to maximize customer value, customer retention, platform profitability, and long-term scalability.

The goal is for every tier to feel like a good deal while creating a natural upgrade path.

Do NOT build plans based only on credits.

Build plans around capabilities.

---

# STARTER PLAN

Price:
$29/month

Target User:
Beginners and first-time builders.

Included:

* Access to AI Builder
* Access to Marketplace
* Access to Blueprint Library
* Build up to 3 active systems
* 500 AI Credits per month
* Credential Vault
* System Health Monitoring
* Revenue Tracking
* Basic Analytics
* Email Support

Restrictions:

* Maximum 3 active systems
* No advanced automation orchestration
* No priority AI queue

Purpose:

Allow users to launch their first online business or automation system.

---

# OPERATOR PLAN

Price:
$99/month

Target User:
Business owners and marketers operating multiple systems.

Included:

Everything in Starter plus:

* Up to 15 active systems
* 2,500 AI Credits per month
* Priority AI Processing
* Advanced System Builder
* Multi-System Dashboard
* Advanced Revenue Tracking
* Tool Stack Optimization
* AI Recommendations Engine
* System Performance Reports
* Faster Build Queue
* Priority Support

Purpose:

Users managing multiple businesses, funnels, automations, content systems, and lead-generation systems.

---

# EMPIRE PLAN

Price:
$299/month

Target User:
Power users, agencies, entrepreneurs, and operators running entire business ecosystems.

Included:

Everything in Operator plus:

* Unlimited Systems
* 10,000 AI Credits per month
* Highest Priority Queue
* Full AI Skill Engine
* Enterprise Automation Builder
* Advanced Revenue Intelligence
* Advanced Analytics Dashboard
* Team Support
* Future White-Label Eligibility
* Future Agency Features
* Future API Access
* Future Team Management

Purpose:

Users building entire automated business ecosystems.
