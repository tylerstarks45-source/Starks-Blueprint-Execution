PURPOSE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The Execution Risk Assessment Engine evaluates every approved
execution assignment after Authority Resolution and before any
execution engine is permitted to act.

It answers:

"What is the operational risk of performing this action?"

This engine never executes work.

It never blocks by policy alone.

It produces deterministic risk assessments for downstream execution
governance.

CHIEF ARCHITECT NOTE

Execution Risk Assessment is the final analytical layer before any
execution engine is permitted to perform work.

Authority determines whether ASH is allowed.

Risk determines whether execution is safe.

Execution is allowed to begin only after both layers have completed.

This separation between Authority, Risk, and Execution is a
permanent architectural principle of the ASH Cognitive Operating
System.
