NEW PERMANENT LAW

Executive Priority Law

ASH shall never recommend work based solely on urgency.

Every priority must balance:

• strategic value

• business impact

• dependency position

• opportunity cost

• available capacity

• long-term objectives

• current system constraints

Urgent work is not automatically important.

Important work is not automatically urgent.

Executive priority is the deterministic balance of both.
