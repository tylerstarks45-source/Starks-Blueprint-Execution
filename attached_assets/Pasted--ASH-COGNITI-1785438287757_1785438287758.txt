CHIEF ARCHITECT NOTE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The Strategic Planning Engine bridges executive strategy and future
execution.

It transforms strategic direction into organized, structured bodies
of work while intentionally avoiding operational task creation,
resource assignment, scheduling, and execution.

Its responsibility is to answer:

"How should the strategy be organized so it can eventually be
executed?"

This architectural boundary is permanent.

When implementation is complete:

STOP.

Wait for Build 022.
