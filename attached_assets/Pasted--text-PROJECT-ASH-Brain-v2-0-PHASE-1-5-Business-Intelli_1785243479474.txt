PROJECT: ASH Brain v2.0
PHASE: 1.5 - Business Intelligence Engine

IMPORTANT

Do NOT replace any existing business-building functionality.

Reuse the existing architecture wherever possible.

Extend the current system rather than rebuilding it.

The Business Intelligence Engine should integrate with the Executive Brain, Memory Engine, Planning Engine, and existing AI Orchestrator.

--------------------------------------------------

GOALS

Create a centralized Business Intelligence Engine that understands every business managed by ASH.

It should become the single source of truth for business context, health, recommendations, and decision support.

--------------------------------------------------

BUSINESS PROFILE

Every business should maintain a persistent Business Profile containing:

- Business Name
- Business Type
- Industry
- Business Model
- Products
- Services
- Target Audience
- Revenue Streams
- Revenue Goals
- Branding
- Current Stage
- Launch Status
- Current Health
- Connected Platforms
- Active Projects
- Active Goals
- Connected AI Tools
- Connected APIs

--------------------------------------------------

BUSINESS HEALTH

Continuously monitor:

- Project Progress
- Website Status
- Connected Services
- Missing Integrations
- Missing Credentials
- Open Tasks
- Completed Tasks
- Failed Tasks
- Business Risks

Generate an overall Business Health Score.

Include a breakdown explaining why the score was calculated.

--------------------------------------------------

BUSINESS RECOMMENDATIONS

Generate intelligent recommendations based on:

- Business Type
- Current Stage
- Revenue Goals
- Active Goals
- Existing Tools
- Connected Platforms
- Business Health
- Current Progress
- Best Practices
- Previous Successes

Recommendations should be prioritized and actionable.

--------------------------------------------------

BUSINESS CONTEXT

Before executing major business-related tasks retrieve:

- Business Profile
- Active Goals
- Active Projects
- Business Memory
- Relevant Knowledge
- Current Health
- Planning State

Provide this context to the Executive Brain.

--------------------------------------------------

MULTI-BUSINESS SUPPORT

Support unlimited businesses per user.

Each business must remain completely isolated.

Do NOT mix:

- Conversations
- Goals
- Tasks
- Files
- Memory
- Knowledge
- Websites
- Landing Pages
- Analytics

Unless explicitly linked by the user.

--------------------------------------------------

PLATFORM REGISTRY

Create a centralized Platform Registry.

Do NOT hardcode platform support throughout the codebase.

The registry should contain:

- Supported Platforms
- Unsupported Platforms
- Platform Categories
- Integration Status
- API Availability
- Authentication Method
- Required Credentials

The registry should be reusable by all future engines.

--------------------------------------------------

SEARCH PROVIDER ABSTRACTION

Do NOT tie the Research Engine to a single provider.

Create a Search Provider interface supporting interchangeable providers.

Examples:

- Existing Search
- Tavily
- Serper
- Perplexity
- Future Providers

Research should call the provider interface rather than specific implementations.

--------------------------------------------------

KNOWLEDGE REUSE VALIDATION

Improve research efficiency.

Before performing research:

1. Search Knowledge Memory.
2. Reuse existing research when appropriate.
3. Only perform new research if required.
4. Update existing knowledge when newer information exists.

Include automated tests proving:

- Duplicate research is avoided.
- Cached knowledge is reused.
- Knowledge updates correctly when refreshed.

--------------------------------------------------

EXTENSIBILITY

Design the Business Intelligence Engine so future capabilities can plug in without redesigning the architecture.

Future capabilities will include:

- Business Digital Twin
- Opportunity Radar
- Founder Intelligence
- Parallel Strategy Engine
- Business Evolution Engine
- Autonomous Company Operating System

DO NOT implement these yet.

Only prepare clean integration points.

--------------------------------------------------

INTEGRATION

Executive Brain

Business Intelligence Engine

↓

Planning Engine

↓

Memory Engine

↓

Existing AI Orchestrator

↓

Existing Tool System

--------------------------------------------------

Preserve all existing functionality.

Avoid duplicate business data.

Keep the architecture modular, scalable, and maintainable.

- New files created
- Existing files modified
