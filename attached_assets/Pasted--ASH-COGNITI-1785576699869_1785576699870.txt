CHIEF ARCHITECT NOTE

The Intelligent Workflow Execution Engine is the final preparation
layer before execution.

Its responsibility is to transform approved execution plans into
complete executable workflows containing checkpoints,
dependencies, rollback paths, validation gates, and conditional
branches.

Execution itself remains the responsibility of the future Execution
Engine.

This separation between Workflow Construction and Workflow
Execution is a permanent architectural principle of the ASH
Cognitive Operating System.

When implementation is complete:

STOP.

Wait for Build 029.
