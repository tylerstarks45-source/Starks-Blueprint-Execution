═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
PURPOSE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The Future State Simulation Engine transforms Executive
Opportunities into multiple deterministic future business states.

It answers one question:

"If this opportunity is pursued, what is the most likely future state
of the business?"

This engine explores possibilities.

It NEVER recommends one future over another.

It NEVER creates plans.

It NEVER executes work.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
MISSION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Enable ASH to evaluate multiple business futures before making
executive decisions.

CHIEF ARCHITECT NOTE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The Future State Simulation Engine is the first system that allows
ASH to reason about multiple possible business futures without
making executive decisions.

It exists to answer:

"What is likely to happen if this path is taken?"

It does not choose the best future.

It does not create a strategy.

It does not create an execution plan.

Those responsibilities belong to the Decision Intelligence layer.

This architectural boundary is permanent.

When implementation is complete:

STOP.

Wait for Build 019.
