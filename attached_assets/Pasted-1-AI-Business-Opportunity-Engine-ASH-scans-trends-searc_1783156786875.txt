One thing I would add that could truly set STARKS apart

I'd introduce an "Empire Mode."

Instead of just building one system, the user says:

"My goal is to build a $25,000/month online business portfolio."

ASH then creates a coordinated portfolio, for example:

Affiliate business
Digital product business
Newsletter
YouTube channel
Lead generation business
AI automation agency

It explains how these businesses support one another, helps build them in phases, and then provides a unified dashboard to monitor and improve the entire portfolio over time.

That shifts the platform from "build me a business" to "help me build and manage an online business portfolio," which is a much more distinctive positioning.

My biggest recommendation, though, is not another feature—it's polish. The feature list is already extensive. If ASH becomes exceptionally good at the core workflow of plan → build → connect → launch → monitor → improve, users will likely find it more valuable than a platform with twice as many loosely connected features.
