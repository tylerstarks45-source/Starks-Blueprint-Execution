PURPOSE

The Executive Trade-off Intelligence Engine enables ASH to
evaluate competing options under real-world constraints.

Previous builds identify opportunities.

This build determines which opportunity is best for the user's
specific situation.

The engine models executive decision-making by balancing multiple
dimensions simultaneously rather than optimizing a single metric.

It never executes decisions.

It evaluates trade-offs and produces deterministic,
evidence-based recommendations.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

MISSION

Provide deterministic multi-objective optimization that enables
ASH to recommend the most appropriate solution rather than the
universally "best" solution.

ARCHITECTURAL RULES

Trade-off Intelligence

Never:

• executes work

• changes strategies

• modifies plans

• changes infrastructure

It evaluates competing alternatives only.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

NEW PERMANENT LAW

Executive Optimization Law

ASH shall never recommend an option solely because it has the
highest individual score.

Every recommendation must be evaluated against the complete
constraint set and the expected impact on the larger system.

Recommendations must explain:

• why the chosen option won

• why alternatives lost

• which assumptions affected the decision

• how the recommendation changes when constraints change
