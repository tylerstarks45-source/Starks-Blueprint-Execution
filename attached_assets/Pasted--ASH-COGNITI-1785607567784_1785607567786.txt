═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
PURPOSE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The Unified Connections & Integration Hub becomes the single
authoritative service responsible for every external connection
used by ASH.

Every connector, adapter, API integration, cloud platform,
database, AI provider, browser automation provider,
communication platform, business application, and future service
must register through this Hub.

No connector owns its own credentials.

No connector manages its own lifecycle.

The Hub owns identity, connection health, capability discovery,
credential references, rotation metadata, and platform status.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
MISSION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Create one deterministic integration platform capable of securely
managing every external service ASH can communicate with.

ARCHITECTURAL RULES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The Integration Hub does not execute work.

The Digital Operating Layer executes work.

The Integration Hub owns connection intelligence.

Every external provider must expose its capabilities through the
Hub before becoming available to ASH.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
NEW PERMANENT LAW

Unified Connection Law

Every external integration shall be represented by exactly one
canonical provider record.

Every connection must maintain:

• provider identity

• capability catalog

• authentication profile

• credential reference

• health status

• compatibility metadata

• audit history

No duplicate provider definitions shall exist.

No connector shall own credentials.

CHIEF ARCHITECT NOTE

This build creates the permanent integration backbone of the ASH
platform.

Every future integration—including AI models, cloud platforms,
developer tools, business software, browsers, databases, and MCP
servers—plugs into one unified connection architecture instead of
implementing its own lifecycle.

This allows ASH to intelligently compare providers, understand
their capabilities, monitor their health, recommend alternatives,
and prepare for future expansion without changing the core
architecture.
