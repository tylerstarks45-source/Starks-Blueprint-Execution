PURPOSE

The Technology Evolution Intelligence Engine enables ASH to
understand how technologies evolve over time.

Technology Relationship Intelligence (Build 034) models how
technologies interact.

Technology Evolution Intelligence models how technologies change.

This engine never upgrades technologies.

It never installs technologies.

It evaluates trends, maturity, stability, lifecycle, adoption,
and future direction.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

MISSION

Create deterministic lifecycle intelligence that allows ASH to
reason about the future of the technology ecosystem.

remain static.
