PURPOSE

The Unified Customer Intelligence Engine establishes the canonical
customer model for the entire platform.

A customer exists once.

Every application references that customer.

No builder owns customer data.

ASH reasons over customer relationships, lifecycle,
engagement, value, and business context rather than isolated
contact records.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

MISSION

Create one deterministic customer intelligence layer capable of
supporting every business model on the platform.
