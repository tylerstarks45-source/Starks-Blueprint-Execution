═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
PURPOSE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The Unified Workspace & Project System becomes the permanent home
for every business, project, application, asset, workflow,
builder, AI agent, and future object created inside the platform.

Users no longer think in terms of files.

They think in terms of Businesses.

Everything belongs to a Business.

Everything inside a Business belongs to one or more Projects.

Everything inside a Project is composed of Assets.

Applications operate on Assets.

ASH understands all relationships.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

MISSION

Create the permanent organizational model for the entire platform.

CHIEF ARCHITECT NOTE

The Unified Workspace & Project System is the permanent home of
everything users build.

Future builders never create their own project structures.

They simply create and manipulate assets within this shared
workspace model.

This architecture allows ASH to understand an entire business
instead of isolated applications, making cross-builder reasoning,
automation, and long-term business management possible.

STOP.

Wait for Build 048.
