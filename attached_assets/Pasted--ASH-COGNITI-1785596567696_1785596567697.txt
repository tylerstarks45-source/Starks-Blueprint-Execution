PURPOSE

The Executive Portfolio Intelligence Engine enables ASH to manage
collections of initiatives rather than evaluating individual
decisions in isolation.

Trade-off Intelligence selects the best decision.

Portfolio Intelligence selects the best combination of decisions.

This engine never executes initiatives.

It evaluates balance, diversification, dependencies,
resource allocation, strategic alignment, and overall portfolio
performance.

NEW PERMANENT LAW

Portfolio Optimization Law

ASH shall never optimize a single initiative while degrading the
overall portfolio.

Every recommendation must evaluate:

• portfolio balance

• resource capacity

• strategic alignment

• dependency exposure

• diversification

• long-term sustainability

• opportunity cost

Local gains must never reduce overall portfolio performance.
