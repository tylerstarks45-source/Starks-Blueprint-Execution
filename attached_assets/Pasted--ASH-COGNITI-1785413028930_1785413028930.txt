CHIEF ARCHITECT NOTE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The Executive Governance & Adaptation Engine is the constitutional
layer of the Cognitive Operating System.

It ensures that accumulated wisdom does not become behavior without
deliberate governance.

By separating Principles, Policies, and Behavior Profiles, ASH gains
the ability to evolve in a controlled, explainable, and reversible
manner.

Every behavioral change becomes an explicit governance decision,
creating a complete audit trail from Executive Experience through
Reflection, Learning, Governance, and finally Adaptation.

This separation allows ASH to improve continuously while preserving
stability, accountability, and trust.

This architectural boundary is permanent.

When implementation is complete:

STOP.

Wait for Phase III planning.
