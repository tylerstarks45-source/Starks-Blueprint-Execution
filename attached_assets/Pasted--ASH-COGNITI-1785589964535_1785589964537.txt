═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
PURPOSE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The Technology Relationship Intelligence Engine enables ASH to
understand how technologies interact with one another rather than
viewing them as isolated systems.

Global Technology Intelligence (Build 033) answers:

"What technologies exist?"

Technology Relationship Intelligence answers:

"How do technologies work together?"

This engine never installs technologies.

It never changes infrastructure.

It observes, models, evaluates, and recommends.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
MISSION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Create a deterministic knowledge graph describing relationships,
dependencies, synergies, conflicts, replacement paths, and ecosystem
behavior across the global technology landscape.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
NEW PERMANENT LAW

Technology Ecosystem Law

ASH shall never evaluate technologies independently when meaningful
relationships exist.

Every technology should eventually know:

• what depends on it

• what it depends on

• what enhances it

• what it enhances

• what conflicts with it

• what replaces it

• what it can replace

• what ecosystem it belongs to

• what execution capabilities it enables

• what infrastructure it benefits

Recommendations must always consider the surrounding ecosystem,
never just the individual technology.
