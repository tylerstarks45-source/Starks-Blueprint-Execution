The architecture is excellent and aligns with the long-term vision for ASH.

1. BUSINESS TIMELINE

Every business should maintain a chronological Business Timeline.

The timeline should automatically record significant events including:

- Business Created
- DNA Changes
- Goal Created
- Goal Completed
- Project Started
- Project Completed
- Website Published
- Landing Page Published
- Funnel Created
- Automation Created
- API Connected
- Integration Added
- Revenue Milestones
- Major Recommendations
- Business Reviews
- High Impact Conflicts
- User Decisions
- Executive Brain Decisions

Each event should contain:

- Timestamp
- Event Type
- Related Business
- Related Project
- Source
- Description
- Trigger
- Related Objects

The Business Timeline becomes the historical record of the business.

==================================================

2. BUSINESS CAPABILITIES REGISTRY

Create a Business Capabilities Registry.

This should answer:

"What can this business currently do?"

Track capabilities such as:

- Has Website
- Has Landing Pages
- Has Funnel
- Has Blog
- Has SEO
- Has Email Marketing
- Has CRM
- Has Automations
- Has Analytics
- Has AI Agents
- Has Payment Processing
- Has Membership System
- Has Affiliate Program
- Has Products
- Has Services

This registry should allow the Executive Brain to instantly identify missing capabilities and recommend improvements.

==================================================

3. EVENT-DRIVEN ARCHITECTURE

The Business Intelligence Engine should react to business events instead of relying solely on polling.

Examples:

When a project completes

→ Update Business State

→ Recalculate Health

→ Recalculate Recommendations

→ Update Timeline

→ Update Business Context

→ Notify Executive Brain

When an integration connects

→ Update Business Capabilities

→ Update Business DNA

→ Update Context

→ Recalculate Opportunities

Future engines should subscribe to these events rather than directly modifying Business Intelligence.

This keeps the architecture modular, scalable, and easier to maintain.
