PURPOSE

The Adaptive Capability Intelligence Engine continuously evaluates
the operational condition of every installed execution adapter.

"Can this capability be trusted right now?"

CHIEF ARCHITECT NOTE

The Adaptive Capability Intelligence Engine gives ASH continuous
situational awareness of every execution capability.

It allows ASH to understand not only what she CAN do, but how
well she can currently do it.

All infrastructure recommendations produced by this engine are
restricted to the Administrator account and are advisory only.

This creates a permanent separation between user-facing operation
and platform self-improvement.

When implementation is complete:

STOP.

Wait for Build 032.
