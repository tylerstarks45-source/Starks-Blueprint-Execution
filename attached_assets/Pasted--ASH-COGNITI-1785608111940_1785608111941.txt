═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
PURPOSE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The Cognitive Certification & Launch Readiness Framework performs
a complete deterministic evaluation of the ASH Cognitive Operating
System.

Its responsibility is to verify that every foundational subsystem,
every architectural law, every registry, every graph, every
connection, every operating component, and every executive engine
is functioning correctly.

This framework never repairs problems.

It certifies the platform.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
MISSION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Provide a complete, repeatable, evidence-based certification
process capable of determining whether ASH is ready for public
operation.
