═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
PURPOSE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The Capability Planning Engine transforms Strategic Plans into a
complete inventory of the organisational, technical, operational,
and governance capabilities required for successful execution.

It answers:

"What capabilities must exist before this strategic plan can be
executed successfully?"

It does NOT create operational tasks.

It does NOT schedule work.

It does NOT execute.

It does NOT acquire resources.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
MISSION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Identify every capability required to successfully execute the
approved strategic plan.

ARCHITECTURAL RULES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Capability Planning identifies what capabilities are required.

Operational Planning organizes executable work.

Execution performs work.

Capability Planning never assigns people.

Capability Planning never provisions systems.

Capability Planning never performs execution.

CHIEF ARCHITECT NOTE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The Capability Planning Engine is the bridge between executive
planning and operational execution.

Its responsibility is not to create work, but to determine whether
the organisation possesses the capabilities necessary to perform the
planned work successfully.

This separation between Strategic Planning, Capability Planning,
Operational Planning, and Execution is a permanent architectural
principle of the ASH Cognitive Operating System.

When implementation is complete:

STOP.

Wait for Build 023.
