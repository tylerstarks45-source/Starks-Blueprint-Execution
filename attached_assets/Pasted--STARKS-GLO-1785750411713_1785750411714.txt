PURPOSE

The Business Experience Builder enables users to create every
customer-facing digital experience for their business using a
single visual system.

This is NOT a website builder.

This is the complete digital experience layer for a business.

Every page, funnel, portal, knowledge base, checkout, landing
page, customer area, and public experience is built here.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

MISSION

Create the world's first AI-native Business Experience Builder
built on the Unified Platform.

CHIEF ARCHITECT NOTE

This is the first true business application built on the Unified
Platform.

The Business Experience Builder is intentionally broader than a
traditional website builder.

It enables ASH to construct complete customer-facing digital
experiences instead of isolated web pages.

Future applications—including CRM, Workflow Automation, AI Agents,
Commerce, and Dashboards—will integrate directly with these
experiences rather than replacing them.
