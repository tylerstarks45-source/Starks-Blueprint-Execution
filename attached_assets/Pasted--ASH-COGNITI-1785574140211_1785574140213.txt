The Execution Simulation Engine performs a deterministic rehearsal
of every approved execution plan before any execution engine is
allowed to act.

"If this execution proceeds, what outcomes are most likely?"

CHIEF ARCHITECT NOTE

Execution Simulation is the final cognitive rehearsal before
execution.

Future State Simulation asks:

"What future should we pursue?"

Execution Simulation asks:

"What happens if we perform this exact action?"

Execution may begin only after Simulation has completed.

This separation between strategic simulation and operational
simulation is a permanent architectural principle of the ASH
Cognitive Operating System.
