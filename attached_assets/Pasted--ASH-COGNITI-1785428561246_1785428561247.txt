CHIEF ARCHITECT NOTE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The Business Analysis Engine is the first system in Phase III that
turns business data into executive understanding.

It does not recommend actions.

It does not create strategy.

It does not execute work.

Its responsibility is singular:

Explain the business as it exists today.

This separation allows future systems—Opportunity Discovery,
Strategic Simulation, Forecasting, and Business Autopilot—to
operate from a common, deterministic understanding of the
business.

This architectural boundary is permanent.

When implementation is complete:

STOP.

Wait for Build 017.
