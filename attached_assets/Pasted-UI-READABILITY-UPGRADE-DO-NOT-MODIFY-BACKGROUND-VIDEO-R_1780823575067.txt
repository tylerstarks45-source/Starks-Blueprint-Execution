UI READABILITY UPGRADE — DO NOT MODIFY BACKGROUND VIDEO

Review the current Dashboard page and make the following UI/UX improvements.

CRITICAL RULE

DO NOT change, replace, blur, dim, pause, crop, remove, edit, or modify the animated background video in any way.

The background video is staying exactly as it is.

Only improve the readability of the foreground UI.

PROBLEM

The current dashboard text is difficult to read because:

Text blends into the bright areas of the animated background.
Some labels are too thin and low contrast.
Statistics and metrics disappear against the moving visuals.
Connection status labels are hard to see.
Footer content is almost unreadable.

The dashboard should feel premium and futuristic while remaining extremely easy to read.

REQUIRED CHANGES
1. ALL TEXT MUST BE WHITE

Convert all dashboard text to:

Navigation menu
Dashboard labels
Statistics
Connection statuses
Cards

Alerts
Footer links

Buttons
Tooltips

No low-opacity text.

Everything should be solid white.

Top Stats Bar
Today Revenue
This Week
Active Systems
Credits
AI Requests
Revenue Section
Systems Fleet Section
AI Activity Section
Alerts Section
Tool Connections Section
Footer Sections

Everything should be contained within readable dark panels.
