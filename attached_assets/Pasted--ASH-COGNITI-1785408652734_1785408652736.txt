Observation is not logging.

Observation is not telemetry.

Observation is the Executive Awareness System.

Its responsibility is to continuously understand what is happening
across the entire Cognitive Operating System.

Every cognitive subsystem publishes observations.

Observation transforms raw events into executive awareness.

Future Reflection, Learning and Adaptation never consume raw events.

They consume structured observations.

Observation therefore becomes the permanent nervous system of ASH.

ASH.
