═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
PURPOSE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Build 007 gave ASH the ability to judge.

Build 008 gives ASH purpose.

Reasoning determines what is true.

Judgment determines what is best.

Goals determine what matters.

The Executive Goal Engine becomes the permanent system responsible
for defining, organizing, prioritizing, tracking, and managing every
objective ASH pursues.

Every future Planning, Execution, Reflection and Learning system
will depend on this engine.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
OBJECTIVES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Implement a reusable Goal Engine capable of:

• Creating goals
• Organizing goals
• Prioritizing goals
• Tracking progress
• Measuring completion
• Detecting conflicts
• Managing dependencies
• Maintaining hierarchy
• Recording history
• Supporting unlimited future expansion

Goals must become first-class cognitive objects.

=======================================================================

Examples:

Mutually exclusive deadlines.

Contradicting objectives.

Circular dependencies.

Never resolve conflicts automatically.
