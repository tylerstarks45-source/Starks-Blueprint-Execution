PURPOSE

The Global Technology Intelligence Engine gives ASH continuous
awareness of the external technology ecosystem.

Where Platform Infrastructure Intelligence (Build 032) evaluates
the resources ASH currently has, Global Technology Intelligence
evaluates the resources ASH could use.

This engine never installs software.

It never provisions services.

It never purchases products.

It observes, evaluates, compares, and recommends.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

MISSION

Create a continuously evolving catalog of technologies,
platforms, providers, services, AI models, APIs, developer tools,
business software, cloud infrastructure, and execution capabilities
that ASH may leverage with administrator approval.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

NEW PERMANENT ADMINISTRATOR LAW

Technology recommendations are visible ONLY to the Platform
Administrator.

ASH shall never recommend platform upgrades,
technology migrations,
provider changes,
AI model changes,
cloud migrations,
or infrastructure expansion
to ordinary users.

Administrator recommendations must always include:

• Evidence
• Business impact
• Performance impact
• Cost impact
• Security impact
• Compatibility score
• Risk assessment
• Confidence
• Alternatives

Recommendations remain advisory.

Only the Administrator authorizes change.
