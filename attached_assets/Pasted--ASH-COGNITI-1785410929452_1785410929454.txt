Reflection transforms executive experience into executive insight.

Observation records reality.

Reflection explains reality.

Reflection never changes cognition.

Reflection never creates learning.

Reflection produces structured Reflection Reports that explain
why outcomes occurred, what influenced them, and what executive
principles can later be extracted by Learning.

Reflection operates on completed Executive Cases rather than
individual observations.

Reflection is therefore ASH's Executive Review System.

CHIEF ARCHITECT NOTE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The Executive Reflection Engine is the first system within ASH that
attempts to understand the complete story behind executive outcomes.

It does not judge success or failure in isolation.

Instead, it reconstructs complete Executive Cases, preserving the
sequence of decisions, actions, observations, and outcomes that led to
each result.

Reflection produces explanation—not change.

By separating explanation from learning, ASH gains the ability to build
a permanent library of executive experience that can later be mined for
wisdom without rewriting history or introducing hindsight bias.

This architectural boundary is permanent.

When implementation is complete:

STOP.

Wait for Build 013.
