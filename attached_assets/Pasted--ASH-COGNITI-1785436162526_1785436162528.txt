═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
PURPOSE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The Decision Intelligence Engine evaluates all simulated futures
and determines which future best aligns with the business's goals,
governance policies, resources, constraints, and executive
priorities.

Unlike Future State Simulation, this layer DOES make executive
decisions.

It never creates plans.

It never executes work.

It chooses the best strategic direction based on evidence.

MISSION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Transform multiple possible futures into a justified executive
decision.
