PURPOSE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The Executive Strategy Engine transforms executive decisions into
coherent long-term business strategies.

It answers:

"How should the business compete, grow, and allocate its efforts to
achieve the selected future?"

It does NOT create execution plans.

It does NOT create projects.

CHIEF ARCHITECT NOTE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The Executive Strategy Engine is responsible for transforming a
single executive decision into a coherent long-term business
direction.

It does not determine which decision should be selected—that is the
responsibility of Decision Intelligence.

It does not determine how work will be scheduled or executed—that
belongs to Strategic Planning and the Execution layer.

Its sole responsibility is to define *what the business should become*
and *how it should compete* over time.

This separation between Decision, Strategy, Planning, and Execution
is a permanent architectural principle of the ASH Cognitive
Operating System.

When implementation is complete:

STOP.

Wait for Build 021.
