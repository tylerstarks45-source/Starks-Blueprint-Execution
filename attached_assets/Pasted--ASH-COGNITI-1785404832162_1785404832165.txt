PURPOSE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Build 009 created executable strategies.

Build 010 introduces controlled execution.

The Executive Execution Engine is responsible for coordinating,
tracking, monitoring, and recording the execution of approved plans.

Execution does not decide what should happen.

Execution performs what Planning has already determined.

Every execution becomes a permanent cognitive artifact available to
Observation, Reflection, and Learning.

Execution must always be deterministic, auditable, interruptible,
recoverable, and explainable.

CHIEF ARCHITECT NOTE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The Executive Execution Engine is the permanent execution layer of the
ASH Cognitive Operating System.

Its responsibility is not to perform work directly, but to coordinate,
track, and manage execution safely and predictably.

By separating Planning from Execution and introducing execution
adapter interfaces, ASH gains the flexibility to execute future work
through AI agents, browser automation, APIs, humans, or other systems
without changing the Executive Engine itself.

This architectural boundary is permanent.

When implementation is complete:

STOP.

Wait for Build 011.
